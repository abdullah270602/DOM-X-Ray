## Scope and mode

The fixture-driven public result viewer is an Experience surface with Operate constraints. The 3D cutaway leads, while status, controls, evidence, and share actions remain familiar and immediately usable.

## Audience, job, action, and proof

Design- and technology-curious visitors need to understand one consequential fact about a captured page, explore the measured object behind it, and share a stable result or evidence-bound local poster. The primary action is START X-RAY. In the local proof it submits only the three visibly named seeded capture URLs through the supervised transport boundary, then loads the immutable scan, scene, result, mapping, and runtime bundle through the same path a public result will use. Published artifact-eligible results expose SHARE RESULT; published neutral results remain link-only; fixture previews never expose public share controls.

## Constraints

The current Gate 3 integration proof keeps arbitrary public scanning disabled until containment is proven. It must preserve keyboard, touch, reduced-motion, and no-WebGL access, render at most 650 counted objects, keep Three.js outside application state, and never relabel a seeded fixture as another submitted URL. Share output must fail closed, use only result-bound public evidence, disclose partial-capture limitations exactly, and never leak target URLs, query strings, titles, selectors, raw IDs, deletion tokens, or hidden fixture data.

## Direction and memorable moment

Instrument Panorama renders the page as a warm-paper architectural section on a physical inspection table. The signature moment is the five-stage Flat to Hero separation ending on a red measurement marker that opens raw evidence. The share drawer extends that same graphite, red, teal, and drafting-paper language into a deterministic 1080 × 1080 cutaway poster with an evidence-derived headline.

## Unresolved decisions

Production public egress, durable storage and retention, abuse controls, production hosting/cache purge, and video generation remain later gated decisions. Local pollable jobs, immutable bundles, stable in-process result routes, a trusted no-network Chromium poster sidecar (strict 1080 × 1080 RGBA non-interlaced PNG) with exact manifest SHA/length binding, same-origin response verification with safe local-export fallback, an exact caption, and a stable share link are now implemented. Client pixel uploads remain unsupported and arbitrary public scanning remains disabled.
